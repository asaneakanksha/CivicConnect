How CivicConnect Works

A simple process from reporting to resolution.

1

Register

Create your citizen account and access the complaint reporting system.

2

Report an Issue

Submit details about potholes, garbage, water leakage, drainage, streetlights and other public issues.

3

Track Progress

Check whether your complaint is pending, assigned, in progress or resolved.

Built for Better Public Services

Everything needed for efficient complaint management.

📸

Photo Evidence

Citizens can attach an image to provide useful information about the reported issue.

🏢

Department Assignment

Complaints can be assigned to the appropriate municipal department.

🚦

Priority Management

Issues can be categorized as Low, Medium or High priority.

👮

Officer Management

Officers can manage assigned complaints and update their progress.

📊

Admin Dashboard

Administrators get an overview of users, complaints, priorities and statuses.

🔄

Status Tracking

Complaint status can move through Pending, Assigned, In Progress, Resolved and Closed.

What Can You Report?

CivicConnect can be used for common municipal and public infrastructure issues.

🛣️

Potholes

Report damaged or unsafe roads.

🗑️

Garbage

Report garbage accumulation and waste-related problems.

💧

Water Leakage

Report leaking pipelines and water-related infrastructure issues.

💡

Streetlights

Report damaged or non-working streetlights.

🚰

Drainage

Report blocked or damaged drainage systems.

⚠️

Open Manholes

Report hazardous public infrastructure issues.
